# Design Refresh Notes

This version is a visual and structural refinement of the existing portfolio rather than a content rewrite.

- Original project files are retained.
- Existing portfolio wording, projects, experience, testimonials, certificates, technology list, CVs and PDF documents remain present.
- The duplicated nested Projects section and prematurely closed Contact section were structurally corrected so the existing content renders consistently.
- `design-refresh.css` is the new presentation layer and loads after the legacy CSS.
- `design-refresh.js` adds non-destructive interaction enhancements: responsive mobile navigation, active navigation state, scroll progress, subtle card reactions/reveal motion, and certificate search/filtering.
- All certificate cards remain in the HTML and their PDF links are unchanged.
- The professional portrait remains `professional-profile.png`.
- Dark and light modes are both retained.
